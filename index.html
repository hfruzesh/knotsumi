Meet the maker

Hi, I'm Sumi.

I make crochet pieces for people who like handmade things with a little character.

KnotSumi grew from a love of yarn, colour, texture and making objects slowly enough that they actually feel human. Every piece is made by hand, and every pattern is designed to be approachable, useful and fun to make.

A few answers about handmade items, patterns and ordering.

More questions? →
Are all KnotSumi items handmade?

Yes. Physical crochet pieces are made by hand in small quantities, which means slight variations are part of the charm rather than a manufacturing defect from Sector 7G.

Where can I buy KnotSumi products?

Products and digital patterns are sold through Etsy. This website is the home base for browsing the work, learning about Sumi and finding new releases.

Are digital patterns available?

Yes. Selected designs are available as downloadable crochet patterns through the KnotSumi Etsy shop.
